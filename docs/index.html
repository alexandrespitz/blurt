Born from a lost dictation

A commercial dictation app froze mid-thought one evening and took a long, careful dictation with it — the audio had only ever lived in that app's memory. Blurt was built the same night around one rule:

Your voice hits the disk while you speak. A crash, a force-quit, a frozen app — none of them can lose what you said.

Kill Blurt mid-sentence, relaunch it, and the recording is found, transcribed, and waiting in your history. There is a test in the repo that murders the process mid-write and proves the words come back.

What it does

🔒 Local, verifiably

Speech recognition runs on your Neural Engine (NVIDIA Parakeet v3 via CoreML). No telemetry, no accounts. The only network request ever made is the one-time model download.

💥 Crash-proof

Audio streams to disk as you speak, through a write-ahead lifecycle. Relaunch converges to exactly one transcript or one recoverable recording — never silent loss.

🌍 25 languages, no switching

Change language mid-conversation; it just follows. English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Ukrainian and more.

🧠 Learns your words — locally

Fix the same mishearing twice in your history and Blurt learns the rule, on your disk. Add names and jargon it should know.

⚡ Live preview

Words trail your voice by under a second while you talk; the final text comes from a clean full pass at up to ~180× realtime.

👀 Gaze Mode

Hands-free: it listens, your pauses cut the sentences, and each one lands in the text box you were looking at. Pairs with any eye tracker that steers the pointer.

Install in 60 seconds

  1. Download Blurt.dmg, drag it to Applications, open it.
  2. macOS will balk once — this is a community build, not an Apple-notarized one. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Grant the microphone and Accessibility when asked, let the model download once (~1 GB) — then it's fully offline. Tap Right Option and start talking.

Suspicious of unsigned downloads? Good instinct — that's why the whole thing is source you can read, each release publishes its SHA-256, and make install builds it from scratch in five minutes.
